⚠️ Facebook Safety Rules & Pitfall Alerts

🚫 Page Blockers
  • Never post the same link more than once per day — Facebook flags it as spam
  • Don't copy-paste the same text into multiple groups in quick succession
  • Never buy likes, followers, or engagement — instant red flag to the algorithm
  • Avoid excessive outbound links in a single post
  • Don't use banned or restricted words (check Meta Community Standards)
⚠️ Slow-Down Warnings
  • Posting more than 3–4 times per day can reduce overall reach
  • Joining more than 5 groups in one day may trigger a limit — spread it out
  • Mass-friending can trigger temporary restrictions
  • Posting in groups too soon after joining gets flagged as spam
  • Overusing hashtags (5+ per post) can reduce reach on Facebook
  • Going Live too briefly (under 5 min) signals low value to the algorithm
✅ Green Light Habits
  • Warm up new group membership: lurk + comment for 1–2 weeks before posting
  • Always post native content to Facebook (don't just share an Instagram link)
  • Respond to every comment — it signals active, quality conversation
  • Use 1–3 relevant hashtags only
  • Schedule posts via Meta Business Suite to avoid manual spam patterns
  • Post at consistent times — the algorithm rewards predictability
  • Vary your CTAs so they don't read as scripted
