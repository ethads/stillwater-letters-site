💡 What is a lead funnel — and why does it matter?

🔴 CriticalWithout a lead magnet, people have no reason to sign up

If someone discovers The Stillwater Letters through Pinterest or Instagram and arrives at your website, the question they're asking is: "What do I get if I give you my email?" A lead magnet answers that question with something genuinely useful — a prayer guide, a devotional, a free resource. Without one, most visitors leave and never return.

🟡 ImportantOne strong lead magnet promoted consistently beats five weak ones

You don't need a library of free resources. You need one thing that solves a specific, real problem for Alice's reader — and you need to mention it everywhere. In your Pinterest bio, your Instagram bio, at the end of every newsletter, in your Facebook posts. Consistent promotion of one good offer is a strategy. Occasional mention of many offers is noise.

🟢 GoalTrack where your sign-ups come from so you know what's working

When you add a lead magnet to the hub, log where you're promoting it. Over time, patterns emerge — "most of my sign-ups come from Pinterest, not Instagram." That tells you where to put your energy. The Lead Funnel section is where you build that picture.

⚡ Start here — log your first lead magnet now

Quick win — 3 minutes

1
Think of one free thing you offer — or could offer — to new subscribers Even a welcome email series counts. "Sign up and receive a 3-day devotional by email" is a lead magnet.
2
Go to the Command Centre, click "+ Add Lead Magnet" and fill in the name Don't overthink it. Even "Welcome email series" or "Free prayer journal PDF" is a valid entry.
3
In "Where Promoted," write where you're currently mentioning it Even if it's only "Instagram bio" — that's a start. Log what's true today, not what you plan to do.

🗺️ What makes a good lead magnet?

The best lead magnets solve one specific, named problem for one specific person.

The difference between a weak and a strong lead magnet
🔴 Weak — too vague
  • "A wellness guide"
  • "Tips for Christian women"
  • "A free ebook about faith"
  • "Subscribe for encouragement"
🟢 Strong — specific & named
  • "A 7-day morning prayer journal"
  • "5 prayers for overwhelmed mothers"
  • "A 21-day stillness devotional"
  • "3-day email series: Finding rest in God"
1
The test: Can someone read the name of your lead magnet and instantly know what they'll receive and why it helps them? If yes, it's specific enough.
2
The best lead magnet for Stillwater is something a tired, faith-seeking woman would download at 10pm on a Wednesday. Meet her where she actually is.

👣 How to add and manage lead magnets

Click "+ Add Lead Magnet" in the Lead Funnel section

A form will open with fields for the name, type, opt-in count, and where it's being promoted. Fill in what you know today — you can update it later.

Choose the Type that best describes your offer

PDF / GuideA downloadable document — prayer journal, checklist, devotional booklet
Email Series3–7 automated emails delivered over several days
Challenge"7-day stillness challenge" — daily prompts by email
AudioA recorded prayer, meditation, or teaching
💡 If you don't have a lead magnet yet, an email series is the easiest to create. Write 3 short emails (200–300 words each) and set them up in MailerLite as an automation.

Enter the approximate number of opt-ins

Find this in MailerLite under Automations (if it's an email series) or Forms (if you have a sign-up form). The number doesn't need to be exact — an estimate is fine. Update it monthly.

List every place you're currently promoting it

Examples: "Instagram bio, Pinterest board description, newsletter footer, website homepage." The more places, the more sign-ups. If the answer is "nowhere," that's your most urgent next action — not creating a new lead magnet.

💡 Your Instagram bio has one clickable link. Make it your lead magnet sign-up page, not your general website homepage. People who find you on Instagram are warmed up — give them a direct invitation to join your list.

Use the Active toggle to mark it as live or paused

If you stop promoting a lead magnet, uncheck "Active" so you can easily see what's currently driving opt-ins vs what's been retired. This keeps the list clean as you build more over time.

📖 Want to go deeper?

  1. Go to canva.com and search for "A4 document" or "ebook template."
  2. Choose a template that feels calm and feminine — consistent with Stillwater's brand.
  3. Replace the content with your prayer prompts, devotional, or journal pages.
  4. Use Stillwater colours: sage green (#3D5A3E), cream (#FAF7F2), gold (#B8924A).
  5. Download as PDF and upload it to MailerLite as an attachment in your welcome automation.

Aim for 4–8 pages. Short enough to feel easy to use, long enough to feel like a real gift. A 21-day journal can be one prompt per page — simple and beautiful.

  1. Log into MailerLite → click Automations → Create new automation.
  2. Set the trigger to "When subscriber joins a group" — create a group called "Lead Magnet Subscribers."
  3. Add an email step → write your welcome email → attach your PDF or link to it.
  4. Activate the automation.
  5. On your sign-up form, set new subscribers to be added to the "Lead Magnet Subscribers" group.

Now every new subscriber automatically receives your lead magnet by email the moment they sign up — no manual sending needed.

  • Create a dedicated pin — "Free 7-Day Morning Prayer Journal — download below" with an image of the journal cover (you can make this in Canva).
  • Link the pin directly to your sign-up page — not your homepage, but the specific page where someone enters their email.
  • Use keywords in the description — "free Christian devotional for women," "morning prayer guide download," "faith journal for beginners."
  • Create 3–4 different pin designs for the same lead magnet and pin them over different weeks. Pinterest rewards variety.
