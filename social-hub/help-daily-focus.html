💡 Why this checklist matters

🔴 CriticalNot replying to comments and messages will tank your growth

Every platform — Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook — rewards accounts that engage with their audience. When you reply to a comment, the algorithm treats your content as active and shows it to more people. Ignoring messages does the opposite. Even a brief "Thank you, this means so much" is enough.

🟡 ImportantShowing up consistently beats showing up perfectly

One imperfect pin a day is worth more than a perfect batch of ten pins published once a month. Platforms reward regularity. If you can only do one thing on a given day, write something or reply to someone — those two actions do the most work.

🟢 GoalFive minutes a day is genuinely enough to start

The checklist has five items. On a good day you do all five. On a hard day, tick two. The habit matters more than the perfect score. Ticks reset each morning so you start fresh — no guilt carried forward.

👣 How to use the checklist day to day

Open the Command Centre at the start of your day

The Daily Focus section is at the top. Make it the first thing you look at — before email, before Instagram. Even 60 seconds of intention in the morning shapes the whole day.

💡 Bookmark the Command Centre on your phone's home screen so opening it becomes as natural as checking the weather.

Tick items as you complete them — not before

The ticks are for you, not for show. Don't pre-tick things you plan to do. Complete the action, then tick. This keeps the checklist honest and useful as a daily measure of what's actually happening.

If you can only do one thing, reply to people first

On days when life is full and you have 3 minutes, go to your email, Instagram, and Facebook and reply to anything waiting. That single action protects your engagement rate and tells the platforms you're active.

Ticks reset automatically the next morning

You don't need to do anything. Each new day starts with a clean slate. Your browser saves today's ticks locally — they're never lost mid-day, but they clear overnight so you always start fresh.

💡 If you want to clear the checklist early, use the "Reset Day" button in the top-right corner of the Daily Focus section.

📖 Want to go deeper?

Habit stacking — link the checklist to something you already do every day:

  • "After I make my morning coffee, I open the Command Centre."
  • "After I read my morning devotional, I reply to one message."
  • "Before I close my laptop at night, I add one pin."

The specific trigger matters less than having one. Your brain builds habits by connecting new actions to established ones. Pick a trigger that already happens at the same time every day.

The fastest approach: use Tailwind or Pinterest's own scheduler to batch your pins once or twice a week rather than pinning manually each day. This way you pin "daily" without being on the platform daily.

  1. Create 10–15 pins in Canva in one sitting (takes about 30 minutes).
  2. Schedule them across the week using Pinterest's free scheduling tool.
  3. Tick "Add pins to Pinterest" on the checklist for the days they go live.

What to pin: quotes from your newsletters, tips, scripture graphics, devotional prompts. All can be made in Canva in minutes.

On Instagram or Pinterest, find 2–3 accounts in the faith, women's wellness, or Christian lifestyle space and leave a comment that is:

  • Specific — reference something from the actual post. "This verse hit me this morning too."
  • Genuine — something you'd say in person. Not a generic "Love this! 🙏"
  • Not self-promotional — don't mention your newsletter or link your profile. Just be present.

These comments are visible to other followers of that account. It's quiet, consistent visibility — the kind that builds a reputation over months.
