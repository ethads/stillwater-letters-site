Stillwater voice — things to remember
  • Warm, honest, never performative. Write like you'd write to a friend you respect.
  • No hype. No "AMAZING news" or "You won't believe this." Real things don't need amplification.
  • Short sentences are your friend. One idea per paragraph.
  • End with an invitation, not a command. "You're welcome here" beats "Click now."
  • The CTA lives at the end, naturally — not shouted in the middle.
  • It's okay to be honest about hard things. That's what makes you trustworthy.
  • You don't need to post scripture every time. Your voice is enough.
