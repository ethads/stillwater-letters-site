How giving works

  • Each button opens a secure checkout page run by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us.
  • Stripe emails you a receipt.
  • Giving never adds you to a mailing list. Your subscription to the letters stays exactly as it is.
  • To cancel a monthly gift or update your card, manage your gift here with the email address you gave at checkout. Cancelling asks no questions. You can also write to alice@thestillwaterletters.com.au and it will be done.

Questions
